      How to read the board

      The big number is the deepest discount on the row: the brand's advertised "up to" figure, or a flagship pick if one is cut deeper. Most items sell for less of a discount than that, so the picks show what you'd actually pay. Deals that have ended drop to the bottom.

      "Select" means only some items are on sale. "Sitewide" means nearly everything is. Carriers show the headline phone or plan price instead of a percentage.

      What's checked, what isn't

      The board is refreshed about twice a day through Cyber Monday (around 7 AM and 6 PM ET): new deals are added, ended ones are marked. Each row links the Canadian sources it was checked against. Product prices are a snapshot from the last check, not live. Stock and prices move hourly during Cyber Week, so confirm on the brand's site before you buy.

      Apple gives a gift card for a later purchase, not money off. Carrier phone prices assume an eligible plan, credit approval and often returning the phone after 24 months.
